## FAQ & Resources

### Frequently asked questions

**Does Card Companion store my data in the cloud?**  
No. All entries live in your browser’s local storage. Clearing your cache or switching browsers will remove the data unless you export it first.

**Can I sync the data to Google Sheets or Notion automatically?**  
Not yet. Copy the tables into your preferred tool after each quarterly review, or use the templates listed below for manual tracking.

**How many cards should I track?**  
As many as you actively use. If a card stays at a zero balance for more than two months and you have no upcoming purchases assigned to it, consider downgrading or closing it to simplify your strategy.

**What valuations should I start with?**  
Head to the [Point Valuation Benchmarks](/valuations/) page for current estimates. If you redeem mostly for cash back, set the valuation equal to the statement credit rate.

**How do I avoid losing my points when closing a card?**  
Transfer or redeem the balance before canceling, or downgrade to a no-fee product that preserves the currency (for example, Sapphire Preferred → Freedom Flex to keep Ultimate Rewards alive).

### Learning resources we trust

- [AwardWallet](https://awardwallet.com/) – Track expiration dates and balances across dozens of loyalty programs.
- [Frequent Miler’s resources](https://frequentmiler.com/resources/) – Up-to-date guides on transfer partners, sweet spots, and promotions.
- [Doctor of Credit](https://www.doctorofcredit.com/) – Comprehensive database of current credit card offers and bank rules.
- [r/churning Reddit Wiki](https://www.reddit.com/r/churning/wiki/) – Community-maintained playbooks, including issuer-specific approval tips.
- [Point.Me](https://point.me/) – Award search engine that surfaces partner flights and pricing so you can validate valuations quickly.
